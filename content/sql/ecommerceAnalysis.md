# Project Overview
This project analyzes a 3-year e-commerce transactional dataset (3,500 records) to evaluate revenue performance, profitability structure, growth momentum, volatility, concentration risk, and seasonality.

The analysis is divided into two parts:
- Part 1 – Business Performance & Profitability Analysis
- Part 2 – Strategic Growth, Concentration & Volatility Analysis (Advanced SQL)

The goal is to transform raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence that supports pricing strategy, product optimization, regional performance management, and long-term sustainable growth.

# Data Source
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sidramazam/e-commerce-sales-performance-analysis

# Overall Conclusion
The company demonstrates:
- Strong structural stability
- Diversified revenue streams
- Healthy and consistent profit margins
- Sustainable profitability

While growth momentum moderated in 2024 and short-term volatility exists, there are no major operational or concentration risks.
The business is positioned for steady, controlled growth with targeted seasonal optimization and margin discipline.

# Tools Used
- PostgreSQL (Advanced SQL analytics)
- Data aggregation & window functions
- Time-series trend evaluation
- Margin and volatility analysis
- Business performance diagnostics
